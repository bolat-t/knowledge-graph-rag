How to read it. Bigger dots share more papers with the selected person; thicker lines are pairs who publish together often. Tight clusters are research groups. Hover a dot for the name; click one to make them the centre.
Every journal article since 2019 with an author at UNSW, the University of Sydney, UTS, Macquarie or Western Sydney — 218,984 works from OpenAlex, updated to August 2026. Similarity search over abstracts (pgvector) finds the papers; a graph of authors, institutions and citations (Neo4j) finds the people and the paths. Connections are co-authorship only.
